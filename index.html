About TiffinTales

Made with love for every working mom in India

🌟 Our Story

TiffinTales was born out of a simple truth — every working mother in India faces the same stressful morning question: "What do I pack in my child's tiffin today?"

We created TiffinTales to make that question easier — with quick, nutritious, kid-approved recipes that can be prepared in 15 minutes or less using everyday Indian ingredients.

🎯 Our Mission

To help every Indian working mother pack a healthy, nutritious tiffin for her child — without stress, without spending hours in the kitchen, and without compromising on nutrition.

💛 What We Promise
✓All recipes are ready in 15 minutes or less
✓Only everyday Indian ingredients — no exotic items
✓Nutritionally balanced for growing children aged 3–12
✓Reviewed for child safety and nutrition accuracy
✓100% free — no subscription, no login needed
✓No harmful ads — only child-safe, family-friendly content
